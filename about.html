About FKLB Engineering Tech

We help inventors, small businesses and fellow students bring product concepts to life. Our work combines technical design with approachable teaching, so beginners can gain practical digital design skills.

What we do

  • Custom 3D CAD modeling
  • Detailed manufacturing drawings
  • Prototype coordination
  • Introductory CAD tutoring

Who we help

Inventors

Turn a sketch into a model that can be built.

Small businesses

Get drawings and prototypes without hiring a full design team.

Students

Learn CAD step by step from someone who studies alongside you.

We are based in Pretoria, South Africa.
